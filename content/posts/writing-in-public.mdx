You don't write to publish conclusions. You write to find out what you think,
and publishing is just the forcing function that makes the thinking honest.

## Ship the draft

A rough note that exists beats a perfect essay that doesn't. Date it, mark it
as a work in progress, and move on. You can always revise in the open.

The archive is the compounding asset. Start it messy.
