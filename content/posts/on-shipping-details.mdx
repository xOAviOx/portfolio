Focus states. Reduced-motion. The empty state nobody sees until they do. None
of it demos well, and all of it is what separates a product that feels
*inevitable* from one that feels assembled.

## A quick list

- Keyboard focus you can actually see
- Copy that reads like a person wrote it
- Loading and empty states designed on purpose

Details don't add up. They *compound*. Ten small rough edges don't feel like ten
percent worse — they feel like the whole thing is careless.

Fix them one at a time. It's slow, and then suddenly it isn't.
