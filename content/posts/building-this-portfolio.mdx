Most portfolios fail in the same two ways: they reach for the nearest template,
and they animate every section on scroll until the whole thing feels like a
demo reel. The fix isn't more polish. It's restraint.

## Pick a point of view

A palette is a decision, not a default. Commit to one idea — here it was *cold
night, warm light* — and let every other choice fall out of it. Five or six
named values are plenty.

## Spend the boldness once

Choose a single element the site is remembered by and keep everything else
quiet. Loud everywhere reads as noise; loud in one place reads as intent.

That's the whole trick. Everything after it is typography and patience.
